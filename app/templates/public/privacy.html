POPIA privacy policy

Compliance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (Act 4 of 2013).

1. Responsible Party

This policy applies to Tshienda Sneaker Care, a student-operated business located at the University of the Western Cape (UWC) Student Centre, Robert Sobukwe Road, Bellville, Cape Town. Tshienda operates as the Responsible Party regarding personal information processed through our kiosk and website.

2. What Information We Collect

We strictly collect only the minimal personal data required to deliver sneaker care services:

  • Customer Full Name: To identify sneaker ownership.
  • WhatsApp Mobile Number: To communicate order progress and collection alerts.
  • Referral Channel: Optional survey information on how you found us.

We do not collect identity numbers, bank account numbers, physical residential addresses, or sensitive personal data.

3. Why We Collect It (Purpose & Lawful Basis)

Under Section 11 of POPIA, we process your information based on your explicit consent given at intake and for the performance of the sneaker cleaning contract. Specifically, your number is used to:

  • Send one-tap WhatsApp status notifications (Dropped off, Washing, Drying, Ready).
  • Verify customer identity during pickup at the UWC kiosk.
  • Notify customers of uncollected items before storage deadlines.

4. Retention & Automated Cleanup Policy

We retain personal phone numbers for a maximum of 12 months following order completion. After 12 months, our automated retention maintenance permanently deletes and anonymizes phone numbers, preserving only aggregated operational metrics (e.g. total pairs cleaned).

5. Data Subject Rights & Deletion Route

Under Sections 23 and 24 of POPIA, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal data at any time.
