How it works in four simple steps

Quick campus drop-off, professional care, and live WhatsApp notifications from intake to collection.

01

Drop off

Bring your dirty kicks to the Tshienda kiosk at the UWC Student Centre. We document condition and give you a QR tagged order number.

02

We clean

Our trained student team hand-cleans uppers, scrub midsoles, treat stubborn stains, and deodorize insoles using eco-friendly solutions.

03

We message you

Staff scan your tag at every stage. You receive instant WhatsApp alerts as your kicks move through Washing, Drying, and Ready for Collection.

04

You collect

Walk into the Student Centre between lectures, show your order code or WhatsApp alert, and step out in spotless sneakers.

About Our Business

"Tshienda" is the Tshivenda word for shoe

Tshienda was founded right here on the University of the Western Cape campus by student entrepreneurs who saw classmates struggling with rain, muddy quad walkways, and expensive off-campus sneaker laundries.

We believe clean footwear changes how you feel walking across campus, attending interviews, or presenting in class. By keeping sneaker care on campus, we save students time, extend the life of expensive sneakers, and create flexible part-time student employment.

🌱 100% gentle hand washing — zero harsh machine spins that damage sneaker glue or foam.
